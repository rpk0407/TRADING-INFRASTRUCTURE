## 6. Immersive Scroll Narrative — Storyboard

The hero centerpiece: **one house, scroll-driven transformation**.

| Scroll % | Scene | House state | Copy / overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5% | Hero | Tired house: cracked facade, faded tiles, single-glazed windows, old boiler smoke from chimney. Grey overcast sky. | "Votre maison, aujourd'hui." — CTA "Scrollez." |
| 5–15% | Diagnostic | Thermal-camera overlay reveals red heat leaks on roof, walls, windows. | "Chaque hiver, 30% de votre chauffage s'échappe par le toit." |
| 15–30% | Toiture | Old tiles lift off, charpente revealed, insulation rolled in, new tiles laid. | "Isolation des combles + réfection toiture." |
| 30–45% | Façade / ITE | Scaffolding appears, ITE panels clad the walls, fresh enduit applied, color warms. | "Isolation par l'extérieur: votre maison enveloppée, été comme hiver." |
| 45–60% | Menuiseries | Old windows swapped for triple-glazed, shutters refreshed. | "Menuiseries performantes, acoustique préservée." |
| 60–75% | Pompe à chaleur | Old oil tank & chimney smoke vanish; a PAC unit slides in at the side of the house. | "Pompe à chaleur — jusqu'à 70% d'économie sur le chauffage." |
| 75–90% | Photovoltaïque | Panels unfold onto the south-facing roof, sun shifts, counter ticks kWh produced. | "Et si votre maison produisait sa propre énergie ?" |
| 90–100% | Renaissance | Sky clears, garden greens, lights glow warm inside, DPE badge flips F → B. | "Votre maison, demain. Avec Bat Iso-84." — CTA "Demander un devis gratuit." |

## 8. Recommended Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 15 (App Router) or Astro 4 (lighter, ideal for mostly-static content)
- **Scroll engine:** Lenis (smooth scroll) + GSAP ScrollTrigger
- **House animation options (pick one):**
  - **Option A — Lottie layers**: AI-generated house frames exported via After Effects / LottieFiles, toggled by scroll progress. Lightest, fastest.
  - **Option B — Three.js + GLTF**: 3D model with morph targets & material swaps per scroll step. Most immersive, heaviest.
  - **Option C — Image sequence**: 120 pre-rendered frames (Google AI Studio / Imagen), played via `<canvas>` based on `scrollY`. Cinematic look — Apple AirPods-style.
- **Image generation:** Google AI Studio (Imagen 3 / Gemini 2.5 image) — generate the 120-frame sequence with strict style-locking prompts.
- **Counters:** `react-countup` or CSS `counter-reset`
- **Forms (quote request):** Formspree / Resend + webhook to info@batiso84.com
- **Hosting:** Vercel or Netlify
- **i18n:** default FR, optional EN toggle

## 9. Google AI Studio Prompt (image sequence master)

Use this as the base prompt, vary only the `state_progress` and `scene_step` to keep visual consistency:

```
A single detached southern-French stone house seen from a 3/4 front angle,
eye-level camera, centered composition, neutral backdrop with soft Provence
sky gradient, cypress trees in background, warm cinematic lighting.
Style: photoreal, editorial architectural photography, 50mm, shallow DOF.
State: {state_progress}  // 0=derelict ... 1=fully renovated
Step: {scene_step}        // tired | diagnostic | roof | facade | windows | heatpump | solar | renaissance
Consistency anchors: same footprint, same windows positions, same door,
same cypress tree on the right. No people. No text. No logos.
Aspect: 16:9, 2560x1440.
```

Generate 120 frames, 15 per step × 8 steps, batch via Gemini API then stitch.
